# Product requirements

## MVP outcome

Help women in Cairo and Alexandria choose a suitable fabric for a desired garment, understand the choice, and buy by the meter.

## Primary journey

Homepage → garment intent → guided questions → inventory-backed recommendations → product detail → cart → checkout → merchant fulfilment.

## MVP acceptance criteria

- Arabic RTL throughout customer-facing surfaces.
- Product discovery does not require knowledge of technical fabric names.
- Prices and stock come from a future verified inventory source, never from generated text.
- Unavailable payment, AI, and delivery services are surfaced as setup dependencies.
